Information collected

If you submit the project inquiry form, the website may collect the information you provide, including your name, email address, company or brand, project type, budget information, and project details.

The website may also process limited technical information needed to operate securely, such as basic request information, security logs, and essential cookies used for form protection and session functionality.

How information is used

Inquiry information is used to review your request, respond to you, discuss potential collaborations, prevent abuse or spam, and maintain appropriate business records.

Analytics

Analytics may be enabled to understand general website traffic and page performance. If analytics is enabled, it is configured separately from the inquiry form and is not used to read the content of your inquiry.

Sharing and retention

Information may be handled by service providers that help host or operate the website, or disclosed when required by law. Inquiry information is kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the relationship, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You may contact Betra Amare to ask about information you submitted through this website or to request that it be corrected or deleted where appropriate.

External links

This website links to third-party services such as Instagram and TikTok. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the website, hosting, analytics, or inquiry tools change. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
